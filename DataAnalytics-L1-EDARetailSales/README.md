# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
Perform Exploratory Data Analysis on retail sales data to understand sales trends, customer behaviour, and product performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset inspection and data types
- Missing value analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Customer age group analysis
- Gender analysis
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Discount vs Sales Amount analysis

## Key Insights
- Sales remained mostly stable during the earlier months, with a significant decline in September.
- Q2 recorded the highest quarterly sales.
- Movies, Toys, and Books were among the top-performing product categories.
- Top-selling products can be prioritized for inventory planning and marketing.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate the reason for the September sales decline.
2. Focus more on high-performing product categories.
3. Improve low-performing categories through pricing, discounts, and marketing.
